I'm an AI/ML engineer who likes the whole journey — from a messy pile of documents to a grounded answer on a user's screen. My work lives where LLMs meet production: Retrieval-Augmented Generation, graph-based retrieval, agent orchestration and the full stack that carries them.

          
At Ericsson I grew from engineer to full-stack developer to team lead, building an internal AI assistant that retrieves knowledge across organizational documentation. Before that, I researched digital twins and failure classification with Volvo CE during my master's thesis — an international collaboration across Sweden, Brazil and Japan.

          
I hold a five-year Civilingenjör degree in Software Engineering (B.Sc. + M.Sc.) from Blekinge Institute of Technology with an AI/ML specialization, and I trained in human-centered design through the global Sugar Network innovation program. Outside work I chair a student nation, connecting students with companies and community.
